GOLDEN DAYLIGHT CINEMA — VALIDATION REPORT FOR EVALUATION BUILD

STATUS: this standalone LUT is available to download, but it is NOT a completed
reference-grade, visually validated release. Full photographic, gallery and
physical-device checks remain outstanding. The only samples used so far are
numerical synthetic colour data. There was no physical iPhone 17 Pro or
Blackmagic Camera test. No test of iPhone downloading was performed.

COLOUR PIPELINE
Float64 analytic evaluation sampled directly on the regular 33^3 input grid.
No 1D shaper or separate conversion LUT is required. Apple Log 2 decode, as
verified against OpenColorIO's implementation:
  P_t = 47.28711236*(0.01+0.05641088)^2
  if P >= P_t: R = 2^((P-0.69336945)/0.08550479)-0.00964052
  otherwise:   R = sqrt(max(P,0)/47.28711236)-0.05641088.
Apple Wide Gamut RGB is converted via XYZ into linear Rec.709. Both have D65,
so this conversion requires no chromatic adaptation. D65 to ACES D60 Bradford
adaptation was used ONLY in the independent reference comparison. At input
[0.5,0.4,0.3], the analytic ACES result matched OpenColorIO's Apple Log 2 builtin
to 4.97e-8 maximum channel error. Primaries and constants are in SOURCES.txt.

Negative linear Rec.709 components are compressed smoothly towards a positive
scene-luminance anchor; negative-luminance inputs are mapped to black. This is
an explicit pre-gamut compromise for exotic Apple Wide Gamut triples, rather
than an assertion they all correspond to real light. The grade uses float64
OKLab and smooth hue weights for blue, foliage and skin/stone; warm bias is
reduced near the skin hue, and colour thins toward shadow and highlight. The
filmic luminance proxy is L^3/(1+L^3), applied as new L = cube_root(proxy).
L^3 is a perceptual-lightness proxy, not physical scene luminance; the shoulder
compresses it, but individual channels can change differently. The out-of-gamut
OKLab chroma is compressed along constant lightness and hue towards neutral,
with a 75% soft knee and a binary-searched Rec.709 boundary. A final numerical
clip guards floating-point roundoff. This is not an implementation of ACES
reference gamut compression and is not a spatial/depth-aware process.

Encode output C = max(linear_C,0)^(1/2.4). Analytic 18% neutral evaluates to
0.45682098 per channel (independent formula check); the baked cube interpolates.

INTERPOLATION (CIEDE2000; seeded Monte Carlo, not exhaustive)
50,000 uniformly random Apple Log RGB values across the entire [0,1]^3 domain:
  trilinear   mean 0.3162, p99 4.6881, max 25.5035
  tetrahedral mean 0.2213, p99 3.0712, max 27.9226
Many full-cube triples correspond to exotic/negative luminance, but the maxima
are real limitations and cannot be dismissed as a guarantee for real footage.
50,000 synthetic nonnegative Rec.709-derived scene colours, randomly scaled
from -4 to +2 stops and encoded to Apple Log 2:
  trilinear   mean 0.2837, p99 1.1388, max 5.4036
  tetrahedral mean 0.1848, p99 0.7998, max 4.9684
These are not measured statistics for actual camera footage.

NEUTRALS, RAMPS AND QUANTISATION
17 sampled grey axis values and 4097-step ramps, first/second difference bounds,
8-bit and 10-bit code counts are recorded in VALIDATION/lut-validation.json. The grey ramp had no luminance reversal. A yellow input-code
ramp had 14 luminance reversals; red, green, blue, cyan and magenta showed no
luminance reversals at this sampling. The blue input-code ramp stays black because
its other channels are fixed at log code 0, representing negative linear input;
it is not a physically plausible blue exposure ramp. These checks do not prove
absence of banding; the 33 grid, quantisation and unusual values can introduce
visible artefacts. The +4-stop neutral ladder shows a small RGB imbalance due
to interpolation. Full per-step grey values and the -4 to +4 ladder are in the
machine-readable JSON. No physical smoothness or display test was performed.

SYNTHETIC SKIN / SKY / FOLIAGE
BabelColor Average dark-skin and light-skin xyY patches were adapted from the
published chart illuminant to D65 and passed through the ACTUAL cube in OCIO.
Under the neutral synthetic lighting at nominal exposure, vectorscope angle
change relative to a scalar-shoulder baseline: dark skin -0.011 degrees, light
skin +0.156 degrees. Across synthetic warm/neutral/cool multipliers and -2 to
+2 stops, the ranges were dark skin -0.42 to +0.40 degrees; light skin -0.84 to
+0.39; blue sky -0.60 to -0.12; foliage -2.29 to +0.90. Angles use atan2(Cr,Cb)
from gamma-coded 709. These limited synthetic patches and improvised lighting
are not verification against a calibrated skin-line target, nor do they verify
actual faces, skies or vegetation. Per-condition RGB and hue results are in
VALIDATION/physical-tests.json.

SHADOW NOISE
Independent Gaussian per-channel AWG scene-linear noise (sigma = 1% of patch
level), through the actual tetrahedral cube, was measured in display-linear
output. Per-channel ratios of output to input sigma: 0.34–0.39 at 0.001,
0.76–1.20 at 0.005, 0.97–1.51 at 0.018, and 1.11–1.69 at 0.18 scene-linear
grey. These ratios compare output Rec.709 channels with input AWG channels. There IS amplification
at several levels. This is not a sensor-noise model or a measurement of iPhone
noise. Noise gain is NOT guaranteed capped; noise reduction cannot be done by a
3D LUT. Independently measured log-code perturbation tests are also in JSON.

NOT PERFORMED
Eight real photographic comparisons, scene-by-scene clipping percentages,
image hue histograms/vectorscope plots, visual refinement on the requested
scenes, real exposure and lighting tests, high-resolution PNGs, charts as images,
gallery, contact sheet, touch/browser testing, phone import/playback and iPhone
download. No Apple Log 2 camera footage or simulation was used. The ZIP is a
LUT-only evaluation package, not the complete photographic deliverable.
A LUT cannot recover clipped highlights or implement depth, grain, halation,
bloom, sharpening or noise reduction.
