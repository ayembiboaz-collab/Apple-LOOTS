TECHNICAL SOURCES

Apple Developer: AVCaptureColorSpace.appleLog2 identifies Apple Gamut primaries
and an Apple-defined logarithmic transfer function:
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/avcapturecolorspace/applelog2

Apple Log 2 white paper is linked by this colour-science issue; we could not
retrieve the actual paper in this restricted environment, so the curve and
primaries were checked against OpenColorIO rather than claiming direct inspection:
https://github.com/colour-science/colour/issues/1428

Academy Software Foundation OpenColorIO, reviewed Apple Log 2 implementation and
sample: same Apple Log transfer curve, new Apple Wide Gamut primaries:
https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/OpenColorIO/pull/2343
OpenColorIO 2.6.0 builtin was also run directly during this build. Primaries:
R (.725,.301), G (.221,.814), B (.068,-.076), white D65 (.3127,.3290).

Apple Log transfer constants and piecewise decoder in OpenColorIO source:
https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/OpenColorIO/blob/main/src/OpenColorIO/transforms/builtins/AppleCameras.cpp

Blackmagic Camera product page: 17- and 33-point 3D LUT support, monitoring or
recording: https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/blackmagiccamera

OKLab (perceptual coordinates): https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/
Colour-science library (CIEDE2000, BabelColor Average ColorChecker patch data,
chromatic adaptation): https://github.com/colour-science/colour
BabelColor ColorChecker data: https://babelcolor.com/colorchecker-2.htm
