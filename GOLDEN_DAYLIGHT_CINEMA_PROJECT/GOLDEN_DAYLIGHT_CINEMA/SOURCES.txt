2. TECHNICAL REFERENCES
-----------------------
Apple Log / Apple Log 2 transfer function and Apple Wide Gamut
  * Apple, "Apple Log Profile" white paper (the piecewise curve used here:
    a log segment plus a quadratic toe below the linear threshold), and the
    Apple Log 2 material that accompanies the iPhone 17 Pro generation.
    The numeric constants used in this project are the ones published by Apple
    and implemented by colour-science:
      R_0 = -0.05641088, R_t = 0.01, sigma = 47.28711236,
      beta = 0.00964052, gamma = 0.08550479, delta = 0.69336945
  * Apple Wide Gamut primaries R(0.725, 0.301) G(0.221, 0.814) B(0.068, -0.076),
    white D65 (0.3127, 0.3290).

Independent implementations used as cross-checks
  * colour-science 0.4.7 - colour.models.log_encoding_AppleLogProfile /
    log_decoding_AppleLogProfile, and RGB_Colourspace for deriving the matrices.
    https://github.com/colour-science/colour
  * OpenColorIO pull request #2343 (Apple Wide Gamut / Apple Log built-in
    transform, APPLE_LOG-APPLEWG_to_ACES2065-1, derived with a Bradford
    adaptation because Apple publishes no matrix).
    https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/OpenColorIO/pull/2343
  * OpenColorIO-Config-ACES pull request #179 (_build_apple_wg_mtx(), the CLF
    transform id urn:ampas:aces:transformId:v2.0:CSC.Apple.AppleLog2_to_ACES).
    https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/OpenColorIO-Config-ACES/pull/179
  * Recommendation ITU-R BT.709 (primaries, D65, and the 2.4 display gamma
    convention) and BT.2124 (dE-ITP).
  * Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license

Blackmagic Camera (iOS) LUT support
  * Blackmagic Design's Blackmagic Camera app documentation: the app imports
    17-point and 33-point .cube 3D LUTs through Settings > LUTs > LUTs Manager
    > Import LUT, and keeps monitoring (display) LUTs and LUTs baked into the
    recording as separate switches.
    https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/blackmagiccamera
  This LUT is a 33-point file, i.e. within what the app documents as supported.


3. VERIFIED FACTS VERSUS ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------------
Verified inside this project (numbers in VALIDATION_REPORT.txt):
  * our Apple Log transfer function is bit-identical to colour-science 0.4.7
  * our Apple Wide Gamut -> XYZ matrix matches a colour-science rebuild from the
    published primaries to 3.3e-11
  * our AWG -> ACES2065-1 matrix matches OpenColorIO's built-in to 4.6e-11
  * the output transfer function is exactly V^(1/2.4)
  * all measurements of the delivered .cube

Assumed, and labelled as an assumption wherever it matters:
  * that "Apple Log 2" means the Apple Log transfer function with Apple Wide
    Gamut primaries (this is what OCIO and the ACES config implement, and what
    Apple's white paper describes; Apple does not publish the matrix, so ours is
    derived from the published primaries);
  * that the Blackmagic Camera app interpolates 3D LUTs tetrahedrally (it
    documents the file format, not the kernel);
  * that the viewer applies a Rec.709 gamma 2.4 display transform;
  * that eight stills represent the range of "golden daylight" footage.

Explicitly not claimed:
  * that any part of this was tested on an iPhone - no iPhone was used;
  * that the LUT is suitable for Apple Log (Rec.2020, iPhone 15/16 Pro) rather
    than Apple Log 2;
  * that the AFTER images contain any effect beyond what a 3D LUT can do.
