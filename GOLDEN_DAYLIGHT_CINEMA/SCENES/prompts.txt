GOLDEN DAYLIGHT CINEMA - THE EIGHT TEST SCENES
================================================================================

PROVENANCE AND WHAT THESE FILES ARE

Each scene begins as an AI-generated photographic still (1672x941, except
scene 08 at 1935x813). Those stills are not camera footage and are not
presented as such. They are converted into simulated Apple Log 2 plates by
encoding them into the published Apple Log 2 transfer function and Apple Wide
Gamut, which is what a camera recording in that mode would hand you.

Reference copies of the eight stills are in SCENES/source/ (1600px JPEGs; the
full-resolution originals are the development inputs, not shipped). The encoded
plates are in PREVIEWS/ as *_APPLOG2_PLATE.png. BEFORE is a neutral Apple Log 2 -> Rec.709
conversion of that plate; AFTER is the same plate through the delivered
GOLDEN_DAYLIGHT_CINEMA.cube. Same pixels, same composition, same exposure.

The scenes were chosen so that each one tests a different failure mode of
midday colour, rather than eight variations of one look.


01  HARSH MIDDAY CITY
    Street-level view down a city avenue, hard 1pm sun, deep awning shadow on
    the left, pale concrete and glass facades, blue sky above the roofline.
    Tests: can shadow under an awning stay open and coloured without the
    concrete going grey-white, and can the sky keep its blue next to sunlit
    walls that want to blow out.

02  MIDDAY PORTRAIT
    Close portrait against a sunlit stone wall, hard light with the shadow
    side of the face still visible, olive shirt, deep shadow behind the
    shoulder.
    Tests: the hardest one. Skin must not go orange, red or plastic; the
    shadow side must keep its colour; the sunlit wall must stay believable
    rather than turning into a yellow wash.

03  MEDITERRANEAN STREET
    Narrow street of honey limestone, shuttered doorways, terracotta pots and
    a strip of blue sky between the buildings.
    Tests: warm stone under warm light without the whole frame going orange,
    plus small areas of real blue that must not turn cyan.

04  BEACH DAYLIGHT
    Backlit sea and sand at midday, sun in frame, spray, wet sand reflections,
    small clouds at the horizon.
    Tests: specular and near-specular whites. The highlight must roll off and
    keep a hint of warm rather than clipping to a flat white sheet. This is
    the scene that shows the LUT cannot invent highlight detail.

05  NATURAL LANDSCAPE
    Mountain ridge, dense green slope, dry grass in the foreground, cumulus
    and blue sky.
    Tests: greens. No radioactive yellow-green, no teal foliage, no
    over-saturated grass, while the sky stays rich and the haze on the ridge
    stays natural.

06  GOLDEN HOUR
    Low sun over a valley, backlit olive tree, dry ground, warm clouds and
    sun flare.
    Tests: this scene should be near its limit. The grade must not double up
    the warmth into orange soup, and the sky around the sun must stay
    believable.

07  MODERN ARCHITECTURE
    White concrete cantilever against a clean blue sky, a large glass wall
    reflecting the sky and clouds, hard shadow edges.
    Tests: pure white surfaces. If the LUT tints white, this scene is where it
    shows; concrete must read as white with density, not cream, and the
    reflected sky in the glass must stay blue rather than cyan.

08  TRAVEL CINEMATOGRAPHY
    Wide 2.39:1 piazza with a fountain, arcaded buildings, crowds in mixed
    clothing colours and strong ground shadows.
    Tests: many materials and many skin tones in one frame, plus a wider
    aspect so the frame cannot be carried by one subject. Judged on whether
    the whole image holds together as a grade rather than a filter.


WHAT THE SCENES ARE NOT

They are not real Apple Log 2 recordings, they do not contain real camera
noise, and they cannot demonstrate how the LUT handles clipped sensor data.
Those limits are stated in README.txt and in VALIDATION_REPORT.txt section 14.
