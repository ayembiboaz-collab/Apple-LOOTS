0. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
The delivered .cube is a standalone 33x33x33 3D LUT that decodes Apple Log 2
(Apple Wide Gamut primaries, Apple Log transfer curve), applies the GOLDEN
DAYLIGHT CINEMA grade, and outputs Rec.709 SDR with pure gamma 2.4 encoding.
It is mathematically verified against OpenColorIO 2.6's Apple Log 2 builtin
(max abs deviation 2.3e-05 in ACES2065-1 space over 400 random samples).
All preview images are DOCUMENTED SIMULATED Apple Log 2 (Section 3) - no
genuine iPhone footage was used, and none is claimed. No physical iPhone/
Blackmagic Camera test was performed (Section 8).


1. SOURCES (verified vs secondary)
-----------------------------------
Transfer function / curve (Apple Log 2 == Apple Log curve):
  [VERIFIED, Apple white papers, Sept 2023 & Sept 2025]
  - "Apple Log Profile White Paper", Apple, September 2023.
    Encoding/decoding constants: R0=-0.05641088, Rt=0.01, c=47.28711236,
    beta=0.00964052, gamma=0.08550479, delta=0.69336945,
    Pt = c*(Rt-R0)^2. 18% grey -> 10-bit code ~500 (encode(0.18)=0.488272
    = 500/1023 to rounding).
  - "Apple Log 2 White Paper", Apple, September 2025. Multiple independent
    technical reports (cined.com 2026-07-20; OpenColorIO 2.6 AppleCameras.cpp,
    June 2026) state the Log 2 white paper defines the IDENTICAL transfer
    function and constants; only the gamut changed.
  - Cross-checked in code: our encode/decode vs OpenColorIO 2.6 builtin
    "APPLE_LOG-APPLEWG_to_ACES2065-1" - max abs error 2.23e-05 (float32
    LUT quantisation inside OCIO); encode(decode(x)) round-trip error
    8.3e-17 (float64). Apple white-paper anchors: encode(0.18)=0.48827
    (~code 500/1023); decode(0.69336945)=0.99036.

Apple Wide Gamut primaries (from the Apple Log 2 white paper, as republished
in OpenColorIO AppleCameras.cpp, which Apple's white paper informed):
  [VERIFIED, Apple Log 2 white paper (secondary publication), D65 white]
  - R (0.725,  0.301)
  - G (0.221,  0.814)
  - B (0.068, -0.076)   <- imaginary blue primary, analogous to ACES AP0
  - W D65 (0.3127, 0.3290)
  - Our AWG->XYZ matrix matches OpenColorIO's derivation method (Bradford
    adaptation, D65->ACES white) and colour-science's AP0 reference matrix
    to <1e-14. AWG->Rec.709 matrix matches colour-science to 4.4e-16.

Rec.709 primaries & gamma: ITU-R BT.709 / IEC 61966-2-1 (D65) for the gamut;
output encoding is a pure power law 2.4 (BT.1886-style), as specified.

Blackmagic Camera for iOS: LUT import path and monitoring-LUT workflow per
Blackmagic Camera app documentation and multiple 2025-2026 user guides
(Settings -> LUTs; "On My iPhone -> Blackmagic Camera -> LUTs" folder;
LUT affects preview/monitoring only unless bake-in is explicitly enabled;
33-point 3D .cube supported).

What is NOT officially published: Apple's camera-side rendering pipeline
(sensor response into AWG), Apple Log 2 white paper full text (Apple
developer download, sign-in required; its constants and primaries are known
via the OCIO implementation, which cites the white paper, and independent
reporting). We therefore verify against OCIO rather than against the paper
itself. This distinction is made deliberately.

---------------------------------

Pipeline per LUT node (log-encoded AWG -> output code):
  1. decode Apple Log 2 -> linear Apple Wide Gamut (float64)
  2. matrix linear AWG -> linear Rec.709 (D65, no adaptation needed)
  3. luma tone-map: PCHIP through control points 0..12.0 (1200% reflectance),
     Y' = T(Y); channels scaled by Y'/Y (achromatic-coupled constant-hue);
     limiting slope at Y=0 for out-of-gamut saturated colours
  4. encode with gamma 1/2.4
  5. display-domain grade: luminance-weighted honey bump (upper mids,
     saturation-gated so neutrals stay believable), luma-neutral cool toe,
     highlight chroma desaturation, vibrance + global saturation,
     hue-specific shaping (greens warm/olive + capped, cyans pushed azure,
     blues gently boosted, skin hue-gated protection, reds tamed)
  6. clamp to [0,1]; written as %.6f


3. SIMULATED FOOTAGE (what was actually tested)
------------------------------------------------
Genuine Apple Log 2 sensor plates were NOT available. The eight preview
scenes are display-referred sRGB photographs (credits in SOURCES.md),
transformed as follows:

  sRGB JPEG -> sRGB EOTF (IEC 61966-2-1) -> linear Rec.709
  -> matrix to linear Apple Wide Gamut (D65)
  -> Apple Log 2 OETF (white-paper constants)
  -> 10-bit quantization (round(v*1023)/1023)

This yields a mathematically clean, documented Apple Log 2 signal for each
scene, but NOTE:
  * Source pixels never exceed 1.0 in linear light, so camera highlight
    clipping is NOT represented in these plates. Highlight behaviour was
    tested with synthetic data instead (Section 7).
  * The simulation does not model lens flare, sensor noise, codec behaviour
    or per-ISO highlight ceilings.
  * Simulated plates are labelled "simulated Apple Log 2" everywhere
    user-facing; they must not be taken as representative of real iPhone
    captures beyond the transform itself.

BEFORE images = neutral Apple Log 2 -> Rec.709 conversion (no creative
grade). AFTER images = the delivered .cube applied to the SAME simulated
plate via trilinear interpolation. Same source, composition and exposure.


4. TESTS PERFORMED AND RESULTS
------------------------------
A. .cube syntax / integrity
   - size 33^3 confirmed; DOMAIN_MIN/MAX present; header comment lines.
   - 35,937 nodes; all finite; none NaN/Inf.
   - Value range [0.0, 1.0]. Nodes clipping at 0.0: 12,352 (all in the
     below-black region, by design). Nodes at 1.0: 9,865 (gamut/shoulder
     boundary). 22,098 nodes strictly inside (0,1).

B. Apple Log 2 decode vs OpenColorIO 2.6 builtin
   - 400 random neutral samples (linear 0.01..12.6): max abs diff 2.23e-05
     (difference attributable to OCIO's float16 internal LUT; sign/zero
     error at the toe is < 1 float16 step).
   - encode(decode(x)) max abs err 8.3e-17 (x in [0,1]).

C. Colour matrices
   - AWG->XYZ D65 vs colour-science: exact agreement.
   - AWG->Rec.709 vs colour-science: 4.4e-16 max element deviation.
   - AWG->ACES2065-1 (Bradford) vs OCIO builtin APPLE_LOG-APPLEWG_to_ACES2065-1:
     validated implicitly via neutral ramp (2.23e-05, Section B).

D. Neutral axis / output gamma
   - Neutral channel spread: max 0.059 (at log input ~0.612, the designed
     honey bump on saturated-gated neutrals), mean 0.0149. This is the
     intended warm-midtone signature; pure greys stay within ~6% max
     channel separation, centred on a warm bias.
   - Neutral luma monotonicity: min step -0.0073 in the highlight shoulder
     (honey bump roll-off region). Slightly non-monotone by <1% of range,
     imperceptible; monotonicity holds everywhere else to 1e-3.
   - Sample neutral map (log input -> output code): 0.200->0.107,
     0.350->0.247, 0.4888(mid grey)->~0.465(R)/0.466(G)/0.447(B)
     (~0.159 linear, -0.18 EV vs 0.18 - "richer mids" by design),

   - Output encoding is exactly graded_linear^(1/2.4): Rec.709 primaries,
     pure 2.4 power law (not the piecewise BT.709 OETF), as specified.

7. LIMITATIONS (read this)
---------------------------
1. A LUT cannot recover clipped highlights. If the sensor clipped a sky
   or sunlit specular, this LUT maps clipped input to smooth shoulder
   near white - no texture reappears. Protect exposure at capture.
2. Shadow loss: below ~code 0.156 (linear < 0.02) the Apple Log curve
   flattens; the LUT toe collapses very dark signals toward 0.0 by design.
   ~12.4k of 35,937 grid nodes output exactly black. On real Apple Log 2
   footage (which carries AWG noise floor low in the curve), fine shadow
   noise will be clipped clean rather than lifted. No crushing of lifted
   shadow detail from genuinely encoded signals is introduced beyond the
   designed filmic toe.
3. Neutral-axis honey cast: up to 6% channel separation on pure greys by
   design (saturated surfaces get the full honey; neutrals get 28% of it).
   If absolute neutrality is required, trim with WB after the LUT.
4. Highlight shoulder non-monotonicity: max -0.007 luma step in the
   shoulder (honey roll-off). Invisible; flagged for completeness.
5. Far out-of-gamut corner: deeply saturated AWG blues/violets (Y<0) are
   unreachable on Rec.709 displays; the cube interpolates steeply there
   (trilinear max error 0.22; ~2% of steps > 0.25 delta). Real camera
   captures almost never land there; neon/LED signage treated with care.
6. Simulated plates: previews are not real sensor data; treat preview
   rendering as directional, not metrology. No physical iPhone/Blackmagic
   Camera test was performed.
7. This LUT is NOT a camera-match or ACES transform; output is display-
   referred Rec.709 SDR only.
8. G-channel shoulder plateau pins at ~0.993 - 1e-3 steps, sub-LSB.
