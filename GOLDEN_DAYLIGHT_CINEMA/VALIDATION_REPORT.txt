     A 3D LUT cannot recover pixels that were already sensor-clipped to white

     - Apple Wide Gamut and Rec.709 share the D65 white point (0.3127, 0.3290);

       bright-room sRGB/Apple Display P3 screens, shadow contrast appears
       slightly richer than a pure sRGB piece-wise curve, as intended for
       cinema-style grading.
