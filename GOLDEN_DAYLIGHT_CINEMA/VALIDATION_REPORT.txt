11. LUMINANCE RESPONSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Neutral axis luma monotonic ...... True
  Smallest neutral luma step ........ 7.74e-05
  Neutral code flat steps .......... 0
  In-gamut off-axis worst step ..... -0.0410 code at input [0.625, 0.6875, 0.6875]
  In-gamut steps losing >0.005 ..... 13.2%

  Measured on decoded linear values, restricted to input colours inside the
  Rec.709 gamut (colours real footage can contain). Along the neutral axis the
  response is strictly monotonic. Off-axis, a rising channel can pull luminance
  down slightly where a channel is being eased into the gamut; this is the
  colour compressor trading luminance for hue at the gamut edge, not a plateau.
  The worst case (-0.041 code) sits at input code 0.63/0.69/0.69: a bright
  cyan (709 = 0.30/0.99/0.96) whose next node along red pushes blue to 1.30,
  i.e. straight through the gamut wall where the compressor must take over.

12. KNOWN LIMITATIONS (read this)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  a) Deep-shadow contrast is compressed, deliberately.
     On the black garment of scene 02 the graded image keeps 45% of the
     local contrast of the plain conversion (std 0.0432 -> 0.0193,
     p5/p50/p95 0.000/0.043/0.125 -> 0.063/0.067/0.118).
     Black cloth at 0.2-0.4% reflectance is lifted to a clean near-black and
     the first stop of shadow detail is flattened against it. This is the
     price of an SDR print that does not crush to 0; it is visible if you
     compare the deepest folds at 100%. It is a design choice, not a bug,
     but it is the weakest region of the transform.

  b) Saturated colours can be non-monotonic off-axis (see section 11).

  c) 33^3 sampling error reaches 0.078 code (p99.9) on the most saturated
     scene. A 65^3 build reduces it further; it does not change the look.

  d) The preview scenes are photographs pushed through a physically based
     Apple Log 2 simulation, not files from an iPhone 17 Pro. The log curve,
     primaries and output are exact; the source distribution is representative
     rather than authoritative. Real footage will contain sensor noise that
     the simulated frames do not have; the shadow lift will make that noise
     more visible than it is in these previews.

  e) Output is Rec.709 gamma 2.4 SDR. Feeding the LUT into an HDR or
     HLG/PQ timeline gives a valid but unintended image.

13. VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The .cube is structurally valid, exact against Apple's published curve and
  primaries, free of NaN/Inf/out-of-range values, free of flat steps on the
  neutral axis, and provably the source of every delivered preview.

  The one behaviour a buyer should know about is the deliberate compression of
